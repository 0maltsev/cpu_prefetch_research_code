## Coverage statement

The matrix assigns an owner and verification method to protocol identity/amendments, queue semantics/provenance, workload construction, factor/capacity rules, schedules and namespaces, producer/consumer timing, lifecycle, private storage, reconciliation, checksums, hardware verification, calibration, roles, 180-cell completeness, replacement, quantiles, effective-tail and zero-loss gates, H1/H2/H3 analysis, access sealing, schemas, and reproducibility. Rows marked `PLANNED_BLOCKED` must not be implemented by inventing their blocking decision.
