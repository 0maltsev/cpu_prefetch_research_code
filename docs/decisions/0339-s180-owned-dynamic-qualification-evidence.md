## Completed evidence and action permission have different lifetimes

The Q15 controller previously returned a receipt without retaining a completed
owner for subsequent scoped admission. Only its actual successful dispatch may
now construct `_CompletedQ15`, after mandatory cleanup and original-deadline
checks. It binds all21 original signed-input, attempt, raw, barrier/result and
terminal files, original seven resolution identities, exact context, image and
environment owner. A dictionary, failed/unexecuted preparation or serialized
success report cannot construct it through the public API.

Every completed proof rechecks the original parent/process, ordered clock/Q15
ownership, protected directory identity/inventory, all original file hashes,
clock proof, native image and unchanged original24-hour window. The original
successful completion must precede its original qualification deadline. Later
admission may inspect that completed evidence after the action deadline; this
does not expose request bytes or permit another action. Alteration or failed
proof latches the owner/environment even if original bytes are restored.
