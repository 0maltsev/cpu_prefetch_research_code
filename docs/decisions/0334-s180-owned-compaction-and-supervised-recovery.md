## Capture and failure boundaries

Check ownership, original deadline, boot and actual zero before family entry,
after receiving a capture checkpoint, before durable parent receipt publication
and immediately before ACK. Retain the first two checkpoint observations in
the parent receipt. All checks are outside the measured loop. A changed control
fails the parent/channel without issuing a continuation ACK, including when
the independently verified archive has already been retained. Latch failures;
restoring the control later cannot resume the family.

Record each qualification/native worker's real PID/start identity, reserved
cgroup and frozen image hash before relying on it for crash recovery. Unknown
live tasks or unverifiable ownership leave the lease occupied. Never kill or
restore based only on an unverified PID or a supplied success report.

## Restoration and recovery

Normal restoration verifies native quiescence, restores hardware/isolation/
maintenance/affinity, then restores the exact sysctl prestate with independent
readback and durable publication before lease clearance. The production
environment-restoration/2 result includes a mandatory compaction result.
Private software transport fixtures retain their original /1 contract; they
cannot satisfy the public production route.

The fixed actual systemd stop-post verifies service ownership, owner death,
boot, capsule and extended lease. Before stopping the original native unit,
verify its original cgroup/keeper and every live native task's protected witness
and executable. Independently restore original platform state before restoring
the control. Bound recovery operations; never reset the operational deadline.
Already completed normal restoration has a separate read-only verification
path, including current hardware/control state and protected platform proof.
Partial, foreign or uncertain ownership leaves explicit quarantine evidence.
Failure to publish additional error evidence must preserve the original error.
