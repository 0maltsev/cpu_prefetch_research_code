# Implementation Decisions

Protocol version: **`2.0.0-pre.1`**. `OPEN` and `RECOMMENDATION_ONLY` are not frozen decisions. A choice becomes binding only through an accepted ADR and, where required, an append-only freeze record. Confirmatory treatment outcomes may not resolve any item.

## Fixed by the scientific protocol

The following are not engineering-choice space:

- Stage A contains packages `R0`, `R1`, `R2`, `L0`, and `L1` over exactly two hardware states, two placements, three residency classes, and three loads: 180 cells per complete block.
- The primary estimand compares complete ring and linked-plus-recycler packages at common logical capacity and matched residency class, not equal byte footprint or isolated pointer dependence.
- Event records are preinitialized immutable one-cache-line objects containing an index, one aligned 64-bit payload, and inert padding.
- The consumer reads index/payload, applies one fixed private checksum update, and records action completion afterward.
- Arrivals are pre-generated open-loop deadlines; each logical arrival receives one enqueue attempt; `FULL` is retained data and separately fails strict zero loss.
- Producer and consumer rows are ordered, private, and independently recorded; join identity is `(run_id, accepted_ordinal)` and record index is validation only.
- Timestamp boundaries, derived equations, the inverse-ECDF quantile, drain inclusion, and no pooling across runs are fixed.
- Warm-up namespace, drain/reset/barrier/start semantics, warm-start interpretation, polling, relax-only waits, and release/acquire termination are fixed.
- Logical row fields, lifecycle states, status separation, append-only raw sources, artifact relationships, and schema version `2.0.0-pre.1` are fixed.
- Genuine low `N_eff` and correctly reconciled full outcomes remain retained and do not authorize repetition or replacement.
- Invalidity permits only a new complete role-compatible block within the frozen replacement budget.
- Block roles, H3 sealing chronology, seven H1 contrasts, twenty H2 contrasts, 270/540 prospective H3 sizing sets, and count formulas are fixed.
- Hardware requested state and verified state are separate; platform claims require readback and evidence.
- Stage B and Stage C are deferred and cannot replace Stage A work.

## Minimum decision set for the next phase

Before Phase 3 build/CI work or production source creation, accept ADRs for: (1) queue provenance/license and reuse mode; (2) language/standard plus atomic feasibility envelope; (3) append-only storage and semantic-validator architecture without yet choosing physical encoding; (4) identity/SHA-256/canonical-record dependency policy; and (5) sealing boundary plus a credible target-platform control interface. These decisions may be investigated in Phase 2; they are not silently frozen by this document.
